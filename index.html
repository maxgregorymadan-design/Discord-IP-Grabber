Operation PowerOFF
OPERATION PO

Welcome to the official domain of Operation PowerOFF. Under Operation PowerOFF, international law enforcement agencies are actively dismantling booter and stresser platforms offering distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack services for hire.

DDoS attacks are illegal. Law enforcement agencies continuously seize booter databases and associated operational data. Anyone operating or utilizing these services is subject to investigation, prosecution, and severe legal penalties.

This domain tracks ongoing operational actions and relevant status updates. Operation PowerOFF continues.
